# Quick reference

## First move per situation

| Situation | First move |
|---|---|
| Page 1 with zero clicks | Title and description in the searcher's words, request indexing |
| Page 2 or 3 | Add depth and proof, link from the strongest pages |
| Page 4 and beyond on a head term | Independent links and mentions, plan in quarters |
| Not in ChatGPT answers | Check `OAI-SearchBot` access and Bing indexing, then the third-party pages ChatGPT cites for the prompt |
| Not in Claude answers | Check Brave Search results for the brand and key pages, submit to Brave, allow `Claude-SearchBot` and `Claude-User` |
| Not in Copilot | Bing Webmaster Tools, IndexNow, AI Performance report, LinkedIn company page |
| Not in AI Overviews | Classic Google ranking first; no special markup exists |
| "Best X company" prompts name competitors | Get onto the lists they come from, with verified reviews; publish an honest comparison that includes them |

## Common mistakes

- Chasing volume on head terms while page-1 queries get no clicks.
- Asking every client for a review without checking independence.
- Treating llms.txt, FAQ schema or a "GEO" trick as a ranking lever; Google states no special markup is needed for AI features.
- Reporting a vendor study's multiplier as a fact.
- Blocking `OAI-SearchBot` or `Claude-SearchBot` while trying to block training (use `GPTBot` and `ClaudeBot` for that).
- Rank-checking by scraping Google.
- Shipping a page that claims something the company cannot prove: AI engines repeat it and buyers check it.
