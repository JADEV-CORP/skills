# Aim higher

Use when the user asks to raise the bar ("be more ambitious", "think bigger", "what would great look like"), or when a plan is sound but small for what it serves.

The three moves come from Felix Rieseberg's essay "How to be more ambitious: a tutorial for humans with models" (2026). Pick only the ones that fit.

| Move | What it means | Fits when | Skip when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Altitude | Aim at the outcome behind the task, not the task as typed | The work serves a goal people will act on | Creative work with no outcome beyond itself |
| Depth | Work the way the best practitioner works: their methods, instruments and raw data | The result must hold up to an expert | Throwaway work |
| Range | Bring in one or two other fields whose methods fit, and say why | The result has to land with people, or the obvious approach has run dry | A narrow fix with one right answer |

## Altitude: build a ladder

Write three rungs, each more ambitious than the last:

1. Understand instead of filling in: work out what changed and why.
2. Get to the decisions: name the few that matter most, with arguments for and against.
3. Replace the ritual: design the better way to reach the same outcome, and show from real history that it would have worked.

Stop at the highest rung that still has an outside judge. Name any setup the rung needs first (access, data, a tool) before promising it.

## Rules

- **Better, not bigger.** Raise the outcome, the standard or the perspectives. Never add deliverables, features or side quests.
- **Method, not costume.** Borrowing a field means using its methods. New vocabulary on the same method changes nothing.
- **An outside judge decides.** A test, a benchmark, a reproduction, held-out data or a measurement says whether it worked. Never grade your own work.
- **Treat the hard target as reachable, and price it.** Assume the ambitious version can be done, then state what it costs in time, money and risk.
- **Real access or say so.** If the data or the tool is missing, name it and stop at the rung that does not need it.

## Output

The task restated at the chosen rung, the outside judge, what changes in the plan, and what is out of scope.
